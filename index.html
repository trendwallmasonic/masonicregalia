Bringing Masonic Regalia Together

TrendWall provides an online collection of Masonic products for members, officers, lodges and organizations looking for ceremonial apparel and accessories.

The store includes a broad selection of products ranging from Masonic aprons and crowns to Fez caps, chain collars, sashes, cases, gloves, flags and other accessories.

The goal is to make it easier to explore different categories of regalia in one convenient online destination.

Understanding Masonic Regalia

More Than Ceremonial Clothing

Masonic regalia includes a variety of garments and accessories associated with ceremonies, offices, degrees and organizations.

Aprons, collars, sashes, crowns, Fez caps and other pieces can each form part of a particular ceremonial ensemble.
